Academic Programs

We offer a wide range of market-driven academic programs designed to equip students with practical skills and theoretical knowledge to address global agricultural challenges.

Undergraduate Programs

Bachelor of Science in Agriculture

Department of Crop Science

A comprehensive program covering crop production, soil management, and agricultural biotechnology. Prepares graduates for careers in farm management, research, and advisory services.

View Details

Bachelor of Science in Animal Science

Department of Animal Science

Focuses on livestock production, breeding, nutrition, and health. Students gain hands-on experience in managing dairy, poultry, and beef enterprises.

View Details

Bachelor of Science in Agribusiness Management

Department of Agricultural Economics

Combines agricultural sciences with business management principles. Ideal for students interested in agricultural marketing, finance, and entrepreneurship.

View Details
